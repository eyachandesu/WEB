Our story

From specialized design to integrated construction.

In November 2013, Engr. Jay-R Chavez founded JP Chavez Electrical Design and Construction, specializing in the design and installation of electrical and auxiliary systems.

In January 2019, JP Chavez Inc. was established as the construction arm, broadening the team’s expertise to include mechanical, plumbing, and fire protection services while JPCEDC continued its focus on design.

Years of shared project experience have formed strong working relationships across the team—the foundation of how we collaborate and deliver.

Our founder

Engr. Jay-R Chavez

Engr. Chavez is the founder and proprietor of JP Chavez Electrical Design and Construction and oversees its nationwide operations. A licensed Professional Electrical Engineer, he has worked on major design projects for leading Philippine property developers since 2004.

His career spans electrical design, site supervision, project coordination, quality management, and leadership. His portfolio includes high-rise commercial and residential developments across Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao, and Cagayan de Oro.

Through continuous professional growth and hands-on industry experience, he leads the organization in delivering efficient, practical, and high-quality engineering solutions.
